How it works

Three calm steps from plan to ready

You stay in charge of your teaching. Lesson Bridge does the heavy lifting.

  1. 1

    Upload your lesson

    Drop in a Word doc, PDF, Google Doc, or paste your plan. Any subject, any grade.

  2. 2

    Match your standards

    Choose your state and course. We suggest the standards your lesson meets — you confirm or adjust.

  3. 3

    Choose the supports

    Select student profiles or accommodations and receive a differentiated version you can edit, print, or share.

Differentiation, done thoughtfully

Supports for the students in front of you

Every class is different. Request exactly the accommodations each learner needs.

🧩

IEP & 504 accommodations

Translate goals and accommodations into concrete lesson changes — chunking, graphic organizers, modified assessments, extended time, and more.

💬

English Language Learners

Sentence frames, vocabulary supports, visuals, and scaffolds matched to each student's English proficiency level.

Standards stay intact

Differentiations keep the same learning target, so every student works toward the standard — just with a different path.

Student support profiles
